Your information is used solely to operate the ShareaSpot service — displaying your posts to other users, enabling social features such as follows and likes, and sending in-app notifications. We do not sell your data to third parties or use it for advertising.

You can delete your account at any time from the account menu. Deleting your account permanently removes all your posts, photos, and personal information from our servers within 30 days.
